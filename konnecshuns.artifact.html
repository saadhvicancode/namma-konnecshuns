How to play

Find the four hidden groups of four. A new puzzle every day.
  1. Tap four tiles you think belong together.
  2. Hit Submit to check them.
  3. Get all four groups before you make four mistakes.
For example
SILK
KEY
SURF
CARD
↓
___ BOARD SILK, KEY, SURF, CARD
Solved groups are ranked by difficulty
  • Easiest
  • Still fair
  • Tricky
  • Hardest, usually wordplay

Every puzzle hides a tile that looks like it belongs somewhere it doesn’t. And you don’t need to be from Bengaluru: nothing here is hard because you have never heard of it.
