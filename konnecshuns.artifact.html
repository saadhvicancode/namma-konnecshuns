How to play

Find groups of four that share something. Bengaluru, Karnataka, Kannada, all of it.
  • Select four tiles and hit Submit to check your guess.
  • Find the groups without making 4 mistakes.
  • Category colours reveal difficulty:
  • 🟨 Straightforward if you grew up here
  • 🟩 Needs a bit of Karnataka
  • 🟦 A deep cut
  • 🟪 Wordplay. Swalpa adjust maadi.

Every puzzle plants a decoy: a tile that looks like it belongs somewhere else. SANKEY is a lake and a Raj engineer. GOKAK is a waterfall and a Jnanpith laureate. Only one arrangement of all sixteen works.
