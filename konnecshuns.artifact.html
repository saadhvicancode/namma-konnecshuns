You do not need to be from here. Nothing is hard because you have never heard of it; it is hard because the tiles are ordinary words and you have to spot which frame they sit in. SILK is the worst junction in the city and also the odd one out among keyboard, surfboard and cardboard. Knowing Bengaluru can actively mislead you. No board is single-themed either, so the grid gives nothing away. Only one arrangement of all sixteen works.
